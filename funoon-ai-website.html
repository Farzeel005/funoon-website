Six steps.
Zero missed leads.

01
Lead Capture

Every inbound message — WhatsApp, Instagram DM, or web form — is captured instantly, 24/7. No lead slips through, no matter the hour.

02
AI Qualification

Wasel converses naturally in Arabic, English, and Hinglish — extracting budget, model preference, timeline, and purchase intent from every buyer.

03
Lead Scoring

Each lead is scored automatically: HOT ready to buy, WARM researching, or COLD early stage. Your team knows exactly who to call first.

04
